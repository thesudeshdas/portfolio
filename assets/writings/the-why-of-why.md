lately, my creative juices have been flowing like a river who has just broken the dam withholding it’s mighty power

> what’s my dam? who knows? i am my own obstacle

I have been wanting to write, make short movies, create software, create ai agent harness, automate my life, do a hundred more things. but why? why should I do any of these? will I make any money out of this? probably in the future. am I good at these? absolutely not. does it look fancy from afar (or as a consumer of these)? hell yeah. do I want fame or be known for these? no. then why?

why does my soul crave to create?

and more importantly, why doesn’t my body let it? I feel like I am playing a tug of war between my heart and mind, one tells to go make something, the other searches for a “why”? if I were to put it in words, this is how the conversation would probably go

---

mind: this ai agent harness can save me 10 minutes of xyz, let’s do this. easy peasy win

heart (interrupting): bro… look at that sky. I think I should start photography

mind: why? spend time on engineering. you get money for this. you can always do photography once you have more money and have a better camera, your phone sucks anyways

heart (dejected): makes sense. let’s get back to work

heart: this bike trip was nice. I’ve shot loads of footage. let’s make a short film. would be nice to capture this for when I am old

mind: are you sure about that? hours and hours of long sitting to learn editing, you are not an editor. you’ve spend 12 hours working, you sure you want to sit longer and stare at a screen? it’s not like you are paid for this. you don’t have a career in this either. anyways, I thought you went on this trip for the sake of ride, not for making a film

---

I can go on and on with more examples on why I don’t do things that I desire to do. but at the end of day, it’s night. kidding, at the end of the day, I need a reason to take that first step and do something, make something, satisfy my soul and not my mind

> side story, I remember once someone called me manipulative, “you are great with words”. the first time, it hurt, like an arrow to the heart. but I spend quite some time with these words until I understood this is a skill I have honed with time and effort. it took me sometime, but eventually, i became proud of the fact that I am good at manipulating people with my words
>
> “sab shabdo ka khela hai”

so, here goes. this is the first step I am taking. I have always loved putting my thoughts in words. I’ve lost more blogs and writings than I have published. but again, why? why do i need to do this?

I honestly don’t have an answer. and doing something for the sake of doing something is not a good enough reason for me

for the longest time my reason for “creating”, doesn’t matter what has been that this is my legacy. I don’t have wealth to pass on to my next generation, but I can leave my stories for my nieces and nephews (and hopefully my kids). maybe few words of wisdom too

but at times this “why” of creating fails against my manipulative mind. why? because I don’t have any nephews and nieces right now. doing something that may or may not exist in the future is difficult in the present

so sometimes even my “why” needs a “why”

I am yet to overcome this part, but for now… I think this is enough

until next time, fin
