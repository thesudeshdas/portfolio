this one is not about tech at all, it is about art

imagine you are a painter.

not a rich, a poor struggling one. you are a great painter, but to fill your empty stomach, you need to work for someone. you go and work at a gallery. you want to paint the majestic alps of switzerland, but the gallery owner sends you to rich mansions to paint on commission. you do it, not with all your heart, but compromises need to be made where do not have the upper hand

you do it long enough that you start enjoying it

one day, the gallery owner brings a fancy looking computer that you can use to paint. he says that now you can paint almost 4 times more. he is genuinely excited about the new machine. he dreams of getting more commissioned deals

listening to your growling stomach, you learn how to use the computer and go on about your tours to mansion

the royal folks enjoy looking at their painting, while some genuinely miss the old brush strokes, their own fear of being left behind in their sophisticated society keeps them mum. they like, or at least pretend to like the painting you make

you never cared about how many paintings you painted, you just enjoyed painting.

you struggle yet, should you quit the gallery and go back to the brush?

come back to reality

take your time in building whatever it is you are building. even if you can do it faster, do it slow. polish it, spend that extra hour that everyone tells is not needed.

it is needed for your soul, not your art, not the gallery, not the draft, but for you

until next time

fin
