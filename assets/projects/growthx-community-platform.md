Built a standalone GrowthX community platform from scratch for member
discussion and connection workflows.

The product includes posts, comments, DMs, search, notifications,
Slack/email/platform notification flows and Claude-based moderation. It was
built as a focused community surface rather than another tab inside the core
GrowthX web product.

Early Mixpanel evidence showed roughly 270 peak weekly active users and 228
unique post viewers in the first month after launch.
